# Preserve uncertainty and changed conclusions without restoring marker ceremony

## Brief

Strengthen Workbench's current instruction to label inference so a reader can
also distinguish unresolved ambiguity, standing contestation,
incommensurability, and materially reduced source-engagement confidence. The
requirement is semantic and local; Workbench need not adopt ARD's exact marker
syntax or closed vocabulary.

Add an equally compact change-integrity rule: correcting an artifact must reach
the downstream claim it corrects, while a materially changed conclusion must
not silently erase the prior research position. Preserve enough lineage for a
reader to understand what changed without restoring temporal-contract enums or
a refresh workflow.

## Acceptance

- Authors mark uncertainty where plain `inference` would misstate ambiguity,
  contestation, incommensurability, or source depth.
- Ordinary directly attested claims remain unmarked; the discipline does not
  create annotation noise.
- Local corrections are reconciled into the affected artifact rather than left
  only in review notes or conversation.
- Material reversals preserve and link the prior conclusion instead of silently
  rewriting history.
- No fixed marker syntax, temporal-contract enum, or re-engagement ceremony is
  required unless implementation evidence shows it is necessary.
