# Make the machinery forecast a proactive design step

Design raises new state and lifecycle costs with the user before a design
binds, instead of leaving the forecast inside the assurance-machinery lens.

Scope:

- A short design step names the trigger (a new or changed mutable authority,
  publication or history owner, recovery path, asynchronous lifetime, or
  cross-host lifecycle) and the proactive conversation: one user action and
  the failure to prevent, three questions, the simplest chain beside the
  proposed one, a recommendation, and a question to the user.
- The assurance-machinery forecast section adds: keep maintenance cost apart
  from compute cost, a synchronous bulk call can avoid an unfinished-work
  lifecycle, preserve current guarantees, reuse settled answers.
- Replace the consuming-project example in the decision-reconciliation
  walkthrough with a neutral one.

Acceptance: design names the step and links the forecast; the walkthrough
names no consuming project.
