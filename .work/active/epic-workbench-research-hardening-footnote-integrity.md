# Restore the no-footnote-fabrication guard

## Brief

Make honest bibliographic asymmetry explicit in Workbench's concise research
discipline. When a fetched source names or attributes a claim to an unfetched
source without supplying title, date, venue, URL, or other metadata, the brief
should cite through the fetched source and must not invent missing reference
details merely to make citations appear uniform.

This sharpens Workbench's existing cite-through rule; it does not introduce a
new citation format or bibliography workflow.

## Acceptance

- The discipline explicitly forbids completing absent bibliographic metadata
  from model memory, even when the recalled detail may be correct.
- Cite-through to the fetched source is identified as sufficient when the
  upstream source supplies no fuller citation.
- Visible incompleteness or asymmetry is described as the honest result rather
  than a formatting defect.
- The rule remains a short authoring guard and adds no operational ceremony.
